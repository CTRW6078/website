About us

Built at Apex Maker Club

Cut The Red Wire is an FTC team run out of Apex Maker Club, a nonprofit in Apex, NC. We've competed in FIRST Tech Challenge every season since 2012.

Our members split into programming, CAD, mechanical, electrical and outreach subteams. Outside of competition, we run an annual STEM camp and hold workshops for younger students in our community.

RIFT

Community

Outreach

Annual MetLife STEM Camp

Every summer we host a week-long camp for 20 campers. Our members teach them engineering, coding and robotics hands-on.

Community STEM workshops

We bring robots to local schools and community centers and run hands-on robot demos and activities for kids. One of these events was featured by Wiley Nickel.
